- Azure landing zones and shared responsibility boundaries

- Layered security, least privilege and anything else that improves the solution's overall security posture
- Ephemeral self-hosted runners and OpenID Connect federated credentials for CI/CD (S14)

## Comparing alternatives (K18)

I will compare the chosen platform design with alternatives such as Azure Verified Modules, Bicep and per-team bespoke builds, using the weighted decision matrix agreed with stakeholders.

## Research methods (K18)

- **Review of vendor documentation and industry literature**, for breadth and established good practice
- **Developer interviews**, to ground requirements in real user needs
- **Proof-of-concept benchmarks**, where the documentary evidence is inconclusive

I chose this mix rather than relying on a single source to reduce bias. I will evaluate how well each method worked in the final report.
