# Proposal requirements

Your project proposal is an overview of what your project will cover. It includes:

- Project title
- Project summary (typically 500 words)
- Stakeholder specification
- Research needs

It also includes logistical information for the EPAO, including contact information, timeframes, and any necessary adjustments.
